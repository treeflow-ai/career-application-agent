# Truthfulness and Evidence Rules

The JD describes the role, not the candidate.

Allowed: reorder, emphasize, shorten, clarify, and use role terminology only when it truthfully describes verified evidence.

Never invent: employer, title, date, customer, technology, domain expertise, years, authorization, clearance, team size, ownership scope, metric, user count, revenue, or quantified impact.

Evidence statuses:
- `VERIFIED`, `USER_CONFIRMED` -> may support factual claims.
- `UNKNOWN` -> ask or record as unknown.
- `UNSUPPORTED` -> do not claim.

Requirement mapping:
- `DIRECT`
- `TRANSFERABLE`
- `UNKNOWN`
- `UNMET`

Named-tool gaps remain gaps unless evidence is added.
