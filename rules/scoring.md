# Fit and Track Rules

Keep three concepts separate:
- **Target Fit**: strategic career direction (1–5)
- **Qualification Fit**: strength of verified evidence match (1–5)
- **Application Track**: PRIORITY / EXPLORATORY / PRACTICE / SKIP

Keyword counts are not final judgment. `UNKNOWN` is not `UNMET`. Transferable evidence may support fit but may not be rewritten as direct named-tool experience.
